Equilo privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short: Equilo has no accounts, servers or ads and uses the internet only if you turn on exchange rates. Transactions, the notifications it reads and your settings stay on your phone, encrypted, and the developer can't see them. Only the exports and backups you create leave the phone, to the place you choose.

Who develops the app

Equilo is developed by AleTos, an individual developer. For any question: AleTos18Dev@gmail.com.

Data the app handles

  • Bank transactions you import from files you choose (statements and exports in PDF, Excel or CSV): date, amount, currency, description, merchant.
  • Payment notifications from the apps you choose only (for example your bank, PayPal or a wallet), and only if you give Equilo notification access: from each one Equilo extracts amount, merchant and date and keeps its text for 90 days, to recognize duplicates and show you the ones it didn't understand. Notifications from all other apps are ignored and not saved.
  • Data you enter: categories and rules, budget, savings goal, recurring expenses, settings.
  • List of installed apps, read only while you choose which apps to read notifications from; Equilo saves only the apps you choose.

All this data is stored only on your device, in a database encrypted with a key kept by Android's security system, and is excluded from Android's automatic backup. It is not sent to the developer or anyone else.

Exchange rates. If you turn them on (Settings → Exchange rates), Equilo downloads the European Central Bank reference rates from data-api.ecb.europa.eu. The request contains only currency codes and a start date, none of your data; as with any connection to a website, the ECB receives your connection's IP address. When off (as after installation), the app doesn't use the internet.

When data leaves the phone

  • Exports and backups you choose. The transaction export (CSV) and the backup are created only when you ask and go where you decide (a folder, another app, the cloud service you use). The backup is encrypted with the password you choose: without it, it can't be opened, not even by the developer.
  • Automatic backup, if you turn it on: Equilo periodically saves an encrypted backup in the folder you choose. To do so without asking for the password each time, it keeps it on the phone, encrypted with Android's security system. If the folder is on a cloud service (for example Google Drive), the file is synced by that service under its own rules.
  • Equilo's notifications (reminders, alerts) are generated on the phone and don't go through external servers.

What the app doesn't do

  • It doesn't ask for accounts or bank logins.
  • It contains no ads, profiling or analytics.
  • It doesn't sell or share personal data.

Permissions

  • Notification access: you grant it in Android settings, after an explanation in the app, and can revoke it at any time.
  • Notifications: for reminders and alerts, only if you allow them.

The Android libraries used for reminders, alerts and automatic backup add standard technical permissions (network state, restarting scheduled work after the phone turns on, running in the background). No file permissions are needed: Android's picker gives Equilo only the files and folders you choose.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the phone until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall it. The texts of the notifications read are deleted automatically after 90 days. Since the developer doesn't receive your data, there is nothing to keep or delete.

Children

The app is intended for adults.

Nature of the calculations

Equilo is not a service of your bank. Budgets, estimates and alerts are indicative and are not financial advice.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page, with the update date.
