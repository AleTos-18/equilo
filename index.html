Equilo

All your spending in one place, and how much you can spend today.

Android app, coming soon to Google Play. Free, no ads, no account and no bank login: your data stays on your phone.

What it does

  • Imports your bank transactions from PDF, Excel or CSV, without duplicates.
  • Records payments as you make them, from the notifications of the apps you choose (bank, PayPal, wallets).
  • Sorts expenses into categories and learns from your choices.
  • Daily, weekly or monthly budget, even starting on payday.
  • Recurring expenses with a calendar and reminders; alerts on unusual expenses, duplicate charges and price rises.
  • Charts, savings goal, widgets.
  • Up-to-date account balance, cash added by hand and any currency, converted at ECB rates if you like.
  • Backup encrypted with a password you choose, automatic too.
